Privacy notice

Applies to the indoor-geolocator skill and plugin, version 2.0.1. Last updated 20 November 2026.

In short

indoor-geolocator is open-source software that runs inside your own Claude session or on your own computer. Its author runs no server for it, collects no data, and receives nothing about you, your photos or your results. There is no account, no telemetry and no analytics in the skill.

To do its work the skill contacts third-party services, and some of them receive the photo you are investigating. This page lists them. Each case report also lists the services that were actually used.

What the skill processes

  • The photo you give it, including its metadata (which can contain GPS coordinates, a capture time and a device model).
  • Text, codes and objects visible in the photo.
  • Public web pages, map data and pictures of candidate places.

Photos can show people or private places. The skill does no face recognition and does not identify individuals. For a private home with no stated purpose it asks what the result is for and otherwise stops at city or neighbourhood level. Wi-Fi passwords, IBANs and names on contact cards found in a photo are redacted.

These services are run by other organisations under their own privacy policies. A photo uploaded to a search engine may be kept or processed by that engine. If that is not acceptable for a photo, tell Claude the photo is private: the skill then uploads nothing without asking, and it can work from text, metadata and map data alone.

What is stored, and for how long

  • A case folder for each photo, where the skill runs: a copy of the photo, the evidence, a report and a ledger. It stays until you delete it. In a Claude chat it lives in that session's files.
  • The similarity model file, in your cache folder, until you delete it.
  • Temporary public copies made for search by link are deleted by their host after 1 to 3 hours.

The skill keeps no profile of you and carries nothing from one session to the next.

Claude itself

Your conversation with Claude, including the photo you attach, is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy. This notice covers only what the skill adds.

Responsible use

Working out where a photo was taken can affect other people. Use the skill for legitimate purposes such as verification, research and your own photos, and not to locate, follow or harass anyone. Results are estimates, not certainties.

Children

The skill is not directed at children and is not intended for people under 18.
